Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

The short version

This site has no accounts, no logins, and no forms that collect personal data. You can read every page and tap every gift link without telling us who you are. Like most websites, we do use analytics and advertising partners to keep the site running — those are disclosed in full below.

What we don't collect

We never ask for — and never receive — your name, email, password, game account, or payment details. Any site claiming to be us that asks for your password is a scam: close it.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful — for example, how many people visit, which pages they read, and roughly which country they visit from. Google Analytics sets cookies (such as _ga) and processes technical data like your IP address, device and browser type on Google's servers. You can read how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on (tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). Our hosting provider may also log basic technical data (such as IP addresses and browser type) for security and reliability.

Advertising

This site is supported by advertising. We may show ads served by third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to your website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com). Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

As a result of ad serving on this site, third-party vendors and ad networks may place and read cookies on your browser, or use web beacons and IP addresses to collect information. Each vendor handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Cookies

We don't set our own first-party cookies. The cookies on this site come from Google Analytics and from advertising partners, as described above. You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings; the site will still work, though ads may become less relevant.

External links

Gift links on this site lead to the game's official claim system, operated by the game publisher. Other external links lead to sites we don't control. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we can't speak for — check them if you're unsure.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, for major changes, note it on the homepage.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us.

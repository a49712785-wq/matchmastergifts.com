Booster tiers, from Bronze to Legendary

🥉 Bronze — starter tier

Basic power-ups, great for learning the game. Mostly earned from daily rewards and early matches.

🥈 Silver — intermediate

Examples: The Slime, Crazy Rocket, Mystery Hat

Noticeably stronger board effects. A solid everyday choice once your collection grows.

🥇 Gold — advanced

Examples: Magic Wand, Laser Beam, Balloon Blast

High-impact abilities that can decide close matches.

💎 Diamond — pro tier

Examples: Doctor Color, Foxy Roxy, Queen Cobra

Rare and powerful. Worth saving for ranked matches and events rather than casual games.

👑 Legendary — the top tier

Examples: Monkey Joo Joo, Billie Boom, Mr. Appleberry

The rarest boosters in the game, often released as seasonal Special Edition (SE) boosters with unique characters. Use them where they count.

📝 Honest note: the full booster roster rotates with seasons and events, so the game's in-game booster menu is always the complete, current list. Tiers above are the permanent structure; individual boosters come and go.

Free boosters FAQ

How can I get free boosters in Match Masters?

Daily gift links (fastest), the Lucky Spin, winning matches, sticker album completions, team rewards, events and tournaments, and watching in-game ads. All seven are covered in detail above.

What is the highest booster tier in Match Masters?

Legendary is the top tier, above Diamond, Gold, Silver and Bronze. Seasonal Special Edition (SE) boosters are character-themed variants that are even rarer.

Which Match Masters booster is the best?

There is no single "best" — it depends on the game mode and board. Diamond and Legendary-tier boosters like Doctor Color, Foxy Roxy and Monkey Joo Joo are consistently among the strongest.

What are SE boosters in Match Masters?

Special Edition boosters: seasonal, character-themed variants (like Monkey Joo Joo and Billie Boom) that are rarer than standard boosters of the same tier.

Can you get Legendary boosters for free?

Yes — from daily gift links when the game publishes them, Lucky Spin jackpots, tournament rankings, and sticker album completions. No purchase required.

Do free booster gift links expire?

Yes — most stop working within a couple of days of being published. Claim them the same day, on the phone or tablet where Match Masters is installed. Links don't work on desktop.

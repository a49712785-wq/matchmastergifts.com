Booster tiers, from Bronze to Legendary

🥉 Bronze — starter tier

Basic power-ups, great for learning the game. Mostly earned from daily rewards and early matches.

🥈 Silver — intermediate

Examples: The Slime, Crazy Rocket, Mystery Hat

Noticeably stronger board effects. A solid everyday choice once your collection grows.

🥇 Gold — advanced

Examples: Magic Wand, Laser Beam, Balloon Blast

High-impact abilities that can decide close matches.

💎 Diamond — pro tier

Examples: Doctor Color, Foxy Roxy, Queen Cobra

Rare and powerful. Worth saving for ranked matches and events rather than casual games.

👑 Legendary — the top tier

Examples: Monkey Joo Joo, Billie Boom, Mr. Appleberry

The rarest boosters in the game, often released as seasonal Special Edition (SE) boosters with unique characters. Use them where they count.

📝 Honest note: the full booster roster rotates with seasons and events, so the game's in-game booster menu is always the complete, current list. Tiers above are the permanent structure; individual boosters come and go.

What are SE boosters?

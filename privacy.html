Privacy policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

The short version

This site has no accounts, no logins, and no forms that collect personal data. You can read every page and tap every gift link without telling us who you are.

What we don't collect

We never ask for — and never receive — your name, email, password, game account, or payment details. Any site claiming to be us that asks for your password is a scam: close it.

What we might collect

Like nearly every website, our hosting provider may log basic technical data (such as IP addresses and browser type) for security and reliability. If we add privacy-respecting analytics in the future to understand which pages are useful, this policy will be updated first.

Cookies

We don't set our own cookies. If advertising is added later, ad providers may use cookies per their own policies, and we'll disclose that here.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us.

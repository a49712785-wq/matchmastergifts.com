How we verify every gift link

Most gift-link sites publish links with zero checks and hope for the best. This page exists so you never have to take our word for it — here is the exact process every link goes through before it appears on our homepage, and the honest limits of what we can promise.

Step 1 — Official sources only

We collect links from Match Masters' official posts on Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram, X, Pinterest, and Discord. We never use "generator" sites, never invent links, and never repost links from random blogs without tracing them to the official source first.

Step 2 — Every link gets checked

Before publishing, each URL must resolve correctly and point to the game's official link domain. A link that doesn't resolve doesn't get published. Simple as that.

Step 3 — Freshness is stamped, not claimed

We record two times for every link: when it was posted and when we checked it. Both appear on the gift card. You'll see "checked 2h ago" — a real measurement, never a fake "updated seconds ago" badge.

Step 4 — Expiry is automatic

Gift links last about 3 days from issue. Each card shows a live countdown, and once a link passes the window it leaves the live list on its own. You will never tap a dead link in our "today" section.

Our honest limits — what we can't verify

  • In-game redemption. The only 100% proof a link works is redeeming it, which would mean playing on your account. We will never ask for your login, so we can't do that — and neither can any honest site.
  • Exact reward amounts before claiming. Amounts shown on our cards come from the official post's text. The game itself confirms the final reward when you tap Collect.
  • Region locks. A few links are region-locked by the game maker. We can't predict those in advance.
  • Whether you already claimed a link. Claims are tracked per account by the game, not by us.

Our publishing rules

  1. No check, no publish. If a link can't be verified, it doesn't go up — even if it means fewer links that day.
  2. Real timestamps only. Absolute dates and times. No relative-time tricks.
  3. Dead links leave the live list automatically. Old links are collapsed under "previous days" for transparency, clearly marked expired.
  4. Mistakes get fixed in public. If a bad link slips through and you report it, we remove it and note the correction within 24 hours.

How often does this run?

Every morning (PKT). New official posts are collected, checked, and published the same day. If the game publishes nothing new on a given day, the page says so honestly instead of pretending.

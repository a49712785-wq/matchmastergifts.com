Best Boosters in Match Masters (2026): Ranked Top 10, Best by Mode & Value

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Quick answer: The best booster in Match Masters depends on the mode — but if you want one short answer, the community guides agree on three names: Billie Boom (Legendary), Sweep It SE and Jelly SE. Billie Boom is the best comeback booster in the game, and the two SE boosters are the only ones that make S-Tier on buspins' 40-booster tier list. There is no official ranking — Candivore has never published one — so every list below is a community guide's opinion, and the two biggest guides even disagree on who is #1. This page merges both, tells you which booster wins in each mode, which ones are the best value, and which ones to save for the matches that matter.

How this ranking was made

A booster is ranked on four things: raw power (how much of the board it changes), versatility (does it work in ranked, events and Rumble, or only one mode), lasting effect (does it keep helping after you activate it), and activation cost (most boosters need 7 blue pieces — a slow, expensive booster has to earn its slot). The two sources are buspins' S-to-D tier list of all 40 boosters and gamersdunia's S/A/B list, both updated for the 2026 meta.

One honest warning before the list: the two guides disagree at the very top. Buspins puts Froggy Fu, Sweep It SE and Jelly SE first in normal matches. Gamersdunia puts Checkmate Charles, Billie Boom and El Magneto SE first. Neither is wrong — they weight different things. Buspins rewards boosters whose effect keeps scoring after activation; gamersdunia rewards raw board control and comeback power. The Top 10 below takes the S-tier names from both lists first, then the strongest A-tier picks, and says which guide backs each pick. If a booster appears in only one guide's top tier, the text says so.

Top 10 best boosters, ranked

1

Billie Boom — Legendary

The best comeback booster in the game. Massive clearing power with chain reactions that generate extra moves, so a losing board can flip in one activation. Gamersdunia's S-tier and its editor's pick; buspins lists it among the four boosters that can get a Booster Power Up. Best in Score Rally and Party Rumble, where big clears matter more than precision. It is also the most common Legendary in the official box odds — 35% of a Legendary Box, against 15% for Monkey Joo Joo — so it is the Legendary you are most likely to actually own.

2

Sweep It SE — Special Edition

Adds 2 brooms that sweep one color to the edge of the board, and the brooms keep working after activation. That lasting effect is exactly what buspins' S-tier rewards. One of only two SE boosters in S-tier. You start seeing SE boosters in the Legends leagues, so this is a late-game pick — but if you own it, it belongs in your ranked loadout.

3

Jelly SE — Special Edition

Creates a jelly area that recolors the pieces you match inside it, and the area keeps paying off for the rest of the match. The other S-tier SE booster on buspins. Like Sweep It SE, it wins by lasting: most boosters do one thing once; Jelly SE changes the board for several turns.

4

Froggy Fu

Buspins' top pick in normal matches, ahead of both SE boosters above. The guide does not publish a full ability breakdown in its summary, so this page will not invent one — the ranking is buspins' call, based on their own matches, and it is included here because it is their #1. If you own Froggy Fu, try it in ranked before you trust any list, including this one.

5

Checkmate Charles

Gamersdunia's S-tier for board control: it reliably creates the Checkerboard special piece, which carries a high multiplier and devastates any board layout. Best in 1v1 modes and Diamond Knockout, where controlling the board beats clearing it. If your style is to set up one big turn rather than chip away, this is your booster.

6

El Magneto SE — Special Edition

Pulls entire rows and columns of objective items toward a multiplier zone. In any mode where specific items have to drop — Showdown, Mushroom Mania — it is dominant, and gamersdunia rates it S-tier there. Buspins rates it A-tier overall, which is still above every standard Diamond booster on their list. The lesson: in objective modes, El Magneto SE jumps the ranking; in pure score modes, it drops a few places.

7

Doctor Color SE — Special Edition

Selects one color and converts tiles to powerful special pieces, setting up massive cascade scoring on your next turn. Gamersdunia's A-tier, best in 1v1 modes and score-focused events. Buspins rates it B-tier on normal days — good, not great — but it is one of the boosters that can get a Power Up version, and a powered-up Doctor Color SE plays like an S-tier. Save your copies for Power Up events if you are in Supreme or Ultimate Legends.

8

Doctor Color — Diamond Tier 3

The best non-SE Diamond booster on gamersdunia's list (alongside Woolly Workout). Converts tiles to one color and creates mass cascade potential — the same idea as its SE version, just without the SE power. If you have not reached the Legends leagues yet, this is the strongest booster you can realistically own, and it is the one to save for ranked matches.

9

Foxy Roxy — Diamond Tier 2

Quick activation, solid mid-game reset. Gamersdunia calls it the best mid-tier Diamond before SE boosters are available, and it is the Diamond most players will actually have in their collection. Use it when the board is stuck mid-match and you need a fast reset, not a miracle — it will not win a lost game the way Billie Boom can, but it wins close ones.

10

Balloon Blast — Gold

The best Gold-tier booster, and the strongest pick until Diamond unlocks. Wide radius explosion, simple to use, and cheap enough to play every day. If you are still climbing through the early ranks, this is the booster to lean on — a Gold booster you own beats a Diamond booster you are saving for a match that never comes.

Best booster by rank and stage

Early game (Bronze and Silver ranks): you do not have the top boosters yet, and that is fine. Balloon Blast carries this stage, and Bronze and Silver boosters come back after a cooldown, so they are safe to learn with — use them freely and learn the boards. Mid game (Gold and Diamond ranks): Foxy Roxy and Doctor Color become your workhorses; save Doctor Color for ranked matches and spend Foxy Roxy more freely. Late game (Legends leagues, 30,000+ trophies): SE boosters open up, and the ranking above finally applies in full. Standard Legendary boosters unlock at 20,000 trophies; SE boosters at 30,000. Your Lucky Box also scales with rank — Arena boxes give Bronze and Silver, Legends boxes give Gold through Diamond 3 — so climbing the ladder is itself the fastest way to own better boosters.

Best value boosters

The strongest booster is not always the best value. Balloon Blast (Gold) is the best value in the game: the strongest Gold-tier booster, usable every day, and it keeps winning until Diamond unlocks. Foxy Roxy (Diamond Tier 2) is the best value Diamond: quick, reliable, and common enough to actually own. The worst value is the opposite mistake — spending a Legendary on a casual match, or buying a specific booster from the shop when boxes give you a random one from the same tier for free. Candivore's own rule is worth remembering: within each booster tier, every booster in a box has an equal chance of being selected, so you cannot force a box to give you Billie Boom — but you can buy a specific booster directly from the Booster Select page when you really need it.

Boosters to save — and boosters to skip

Save for ranked matches and tournaments: Billie Boom, Sweep It SE, Jelly SE, Checkmate Charles, El Magneto SE. These are rare, and their best modes are the ones with the best rewards. Skip or spend freely: Bronze and Silver boosters (they come back after a cooldown), and any D-tier booster with a small, random or one-off effect — useful for learning, not for deciding a match. And a special case: four boosters get much stronger during a Booster Power Up event — UFO SE, Vinnie Valentine SE, Foxy Roxy SE and Billie Boom — so if you own one of those, save your copies for the event instead of spending them on normal days. Power Up events only run for Supreme Legends and Ultimate Legends players, so below those leagues the advice is simple: spend what you have, and save only the top five above.

⚠️ Power Up is league-gated: Booster Power Up events only run for players in Supreme Legends and Ultimate Legends. If you are below those leagues, do not hold boosters waiting for an event that will not come to you yet.

Best boosters FAQ

Which is the best booster in Match Masters?

There is no official best — Candivore has never ranked them. The community consensus is Billie Boom (Legendary), Sweep It SE and Jelly SE, with Froggy Fu and Checkmate Charles also rated number one by one guide each. Pick from the Top 10 by your game mode, not by the list order alone.

What does S-tier mean?

It is a community ranking, not an official grade. On buspins' list, S-tier means the booster's effect keeps helping after activation — brooms, lotus flowers, a jelly area. A-tier is strong board control with a follow-up effect. B-tier is powerful but situational. C-tier is reliable but rarely decides a match. D-tier is small, random or one-off effects.

Are SE boosters better than Legendary boosters?

Usually, but not always. Only two of the eight SE boosters make S-tier (Sweep It SE and Jelly SE), and some SE boosters are only average on normal days. A Legendary like Billie Boom beats most SE boosters in most modes — and during a Power Up event, a powered-up SE or Billie Boom beats everything.

Which booster should beginners use?

Balloon Blast (Gold) until Diamond unlocks, then Foxy Roxy. Both are strong, simple to use, and common enough to own. Save your first Diamond and Legendary boosters for ranked matches instead of spending them while you are still learning the boards.

How do I get Billie Boom for free?

The same free sources as any booster — gift links, Lucky Spin, events, tournaments, album completions — and it is the most common Legendary in the official box odds (35% of a Legendary Box, against 15% for Monkey Joo Joo). There is no way to force a box to give you a specific booster; within a tier, every booster has an equal chance.

Do boosters work in tournaments?

Yes — but your booster and perks lock for the whole tournament once you enter, and one loss ends your run. Pick a booster you trust on any board, not one that needs a perfect setup. Diamond and Legendary only.

Which booster is best for saving coins?

Balloon Blast and Foxy Roxy: strong enough to win, cheap enough to sustain. The expensive mistake is the other way round — burning a Legendary on a casual match, or buying a specific booster from the shop when a free box might give you one from the same tier.

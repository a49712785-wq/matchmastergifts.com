How stickers and albums work

Stickers are collectibles you place into albums. Every sticker has its own slot on an album page, and you win a prize when you finish a page — and a much bigger prize when you finish the whole album. The Albums tab unlocks early in the game (community guides put it at around 200 trophies), and your first sticker can come from the Trophy Road, a gift link, or a pack.

There are three kinds of albums. Regular albums are the main ones — they never expire, so there is no rush, only patience. Seasonal albums are time-limited: each one runs for about a month (one league season) and closes when the season ends, so an unfinished Seasonal album is a lost reward, not a postponed one. Solo albums work differently — they never give you duplicate stickers, which makes them the most predictable albums in the game. One rule catches almost every player eventually: if you have too many incomplete albums, the game will not open a new one. The fix is always the same — finish the album closest to completion first, and the next one opens.

8 free ways to get stickers

1

Daily gift links

The fastest source. The game publishes free gift links on its official social pages most days, and sticker links are among the most common — we verify and publish them every morning on today's gift list. Each link works once per account and expires in about 3 days, so claim it the day you see it.

2

Lucky Spin

One free spin on the in-game wheel every day. The wheel pays coins, boosters and sticker packs — small odds per spin, but it costs nothing and it never misses a day.

3

Sticker packs from the shop

Spend the coins you earn by winning matches on sticker packs. This is the one method that costs coins — but coins are free if you play, and our free coins guide covers every way to refill them. Do not buy packs with real money for album completion; the odds above make that a bad deal.

4

Events and tournaments

Rotating events pay sticker packs along their milestone tracks, and the final tiers of bigger events often include packs or tokens. Play the events you would play anyway — the stickers are a bonus, not the reason to enter.

5

Ask your teammates

The most underused button in the game. In the album, TRADE → REQUEST builds one link covering every sticker you are missing, and ASK TEAM asks your teammates directly. Players can only send you stickers you are missing — so every sticker you get this way is new. If you are not in an active team yet, joining one is worth more for albums than any pack.

6

Trade duplicates

Your duplicate Whites and Silvers are currency. Open the album, tap a sticker slot, choose Trade, and offer your duplicates for the stickers you need — or create a Trade Link and share it in the official Match Masters trading group on Facebook. Full rules in the next section.

7

Missing Sticker rewards

Some rewards carry a "Missing Sticker" tag — they give you only stickers you need, never duplicates. These are the best sticker rewards in the game for finishing albums, so use them when you see them and do not waste them on an album you barely started.

8

Reward keys

Keys dropped in livestreams and social posts sometimes pay sticker packs. They are irregular, they expire fast, and they are worth checking — current keys live on our reward keys page.

Trading rules (read before you trade)

Trading is where albums are actually finished — but it has hard limits, and the Gold rule ruins a lot of players' weeks.

WhatRule
White stickersTrade or send up to 2 per day, any day
Silver stickersTrade or send up to 1 per day, any day
Gold stickersSpecial trading days only — Sunday is the best-known Gold trading day. Miss it and you wait a full week.
Diamond stickersSpecial trading days only
Seasonal stickersSpecial trading days only — and the season ends whether you finished or not

How to trade: open the album, tap the sticker slot, tap Trade, then either request a specific sticker or offer your duplicates. For a wider audience, tap "New Trade", select the stickers you want to send, and create a Trade Link — share it in the official trading group on Facebook. Two habits matter more than anything: never hold Gold duplicates past the trading day (they are useless until the next one), and set a weekly Sunday reminder for Gold trades. White and Silver duplicates should move constantly — steady trading builds goodwill in your team, and goodwill is what gets your requests answered.

⚠️ Trade safely: only trade inside the game or through official Trade Links. Anyone asking for your login, a payment, or an off-game "verification" step to complete a trade is running a scam — real trades never need any of those.

Duplicates are never wasted

Every duplicate sticker you pull adds points to a reward bar called the Duplicates Bar — from 1 point for a White sticker up to 50 points for a Diamond one. The bar pays out rewards as it fills, so duplicates are a slow, guaranteed income stream rather than trash. Between the bar and trading, there is no such thing as a useless duplicate: trade the ones someone needs, and let the rest feed the bar. The only mistake is letting duplicates sit there doing nothing.

Sticker Tokens: save them for the hard ones

A Sticker Token is a wildcard — it turns into any missing sticker you choose. That makes it the most valuable single item in the album system, and the most commonly wasted one. The rule is simple: never spend a token on a White or Silver sticker. Those arrive on their own through packs, links and trades. Spend tokens only on the stickers you cannot get any other way — a Diamond sticker that has eluded you for weeks, or a Seasonal sticker you need before the league season ends. A token spent on a common sticker is a token thrown away.

How to complete albums faster

The routine that actually works

  1. Finish one album at a time. Completion rewards come page by page, not from partial progress spread across five albums — and a finished album is what opens the next one.
  2. Start with the album closest to completion. Two missing stickers beats ten, every time.
  3. Ask every day. TRADE → REQUEST and ASK TEAM cost nothing. Players can only send you stickers you are missing, so every ask is pure progress.
  4. Trade your duplicates the same day you get them. White and Silver move daily; Gold moves on Sunday. A duplicate sitting in your collection helps nobody.
  5. Save Sticker Tokens for Diamond and Seasonal stickers. See above — this is the single biggest difference between fast and slow completers.
  6. Watch the Seasonal deadline. A Seasonal album that is 90% done when the season ends pays nothing. If a Seasonal album is close, it jumps the queue ahead of every Regular album.
  7. Claim gift links every morning. Sticker links are frequent, they expire in about 3 days, and they are the only source that asks nothing of you at all.

What album completion actually pays

Albums are one of the most rewarding systems in the game precisely because they pay at every step. Completing a page pays boosters, coins, chests and sometimes exclusive outfits. Completing a whole album pays a mega prize bundle that can include Legendary and Special Edition boosters, Super Spins, Sticker Tokens, big coin prizes, emotes, and outfits you can only win through albums. That is why the album grind is worth it: the final bundle is one of the few places in the game where top-tier boosters arrive without spending coins or real money. And every album you finish also feeds the cycle — new albums open, new pages pay, and your booster collection grows.

Mistakes that slow you down

1

Spending Sticker Tokens on common stickers

White and Silver stickers arrive on their own. A token spent on one is gone forever — save it for Diamond or Seasonal.

2

Ignoring the Sunday Gold window

Gold stickers trade on special days only. Miss Sunday and your Gold duplicates sit useless for a full week.

3

Spreading across too many albums

Five half-finished albums pay nothing and block new ones from opening. One album at a time, closest to completion first.

4

Playing without a team

ASK TEAM and teammate trades are the fastest reliable source of specific missing stickers. Solo players finish albums far slower.

5

Letting gift links expire

Sticker links are frequent and free — but they die in about 3 days. Check every morning; a missed link is a missed sticker.

Free stickers FAQ

How do I get free stickers in Match Masters?

Daily gift links, the Lucky Spin, sticker packs bought with coins, event rewards, asking teammates, and trading duplicates. Gift links are the fastest source — claim them the same day, because they expire in about 3 days.

When can you trade Gold stickers in Match Masters?

Gold stickers can only be traded on special trading days — Sunday is the best-known Gold trading day. White and Silver stickers can be traded any day (up to 2 White and 1 Silver per day). Never hold Gold duplicates past the trading day.

What are Sticker Tokens in Match Masters?

A Sticker Token is a wildcard that turns into any missing sticker you choose. Save it for the hardest stickers to find — usually a Diamond sticker or a Seasonal sticker you need before the season ends.

What happens to duplicate stickers in Match Masters?

Duplicates are never wasted. Each one adds points to the Duplicates Bar — from 1 point for a White sticker up to 50 for a Diamond — and the bar pays out rewards as it fills. Duplicates can also be traded to teammates or other players.

What do you get for completing a sticker album?

Each completed page pays boosters, coins, chests and sometimes exclusive outfits. Completing a whole album pays a mega prize bundle that can include Legendary and Special Edition boosters, Super Spins, Sticker Tokens, big coin prizes, emotes and outfits you can only win through albums.

Why won't a new sticker album open in Match Masters?

If you have too many incomplete albums, the game stops opening new ones. Finish the album closest to completion first, and a new one will open. Seasonal albums also close when the league season ends, so they have a hard deadline.

Do Match Masters sticker links expire?

Yes — gift links that give stickers expire in about 3 days, the same as every other gift link. Claim them the day they appear, on the phone or tablet where Match Masters is installed.

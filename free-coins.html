Quick answers

Why do I keep running out of coins?

Because every booster equipped costs coins, and most players equip one every match. If you play a lot of matches, coins drain faster than casual sources refill them — that's exactly what the daily routine above fixes.

Should I spend coins on the Lucky Spin?

Only when you're out of free spins and the wheel has a boosted event running. Otherwise save coins for boosters and tournament entries, which have better returns.

Are "unlimited coin" generators real?

No. Every single one is a scam — they harvest your login or install malware. Real free coins only come from the game itself: links, spins, wins, and events.

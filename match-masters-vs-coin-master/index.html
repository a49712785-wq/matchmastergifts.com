What is Match Masters?

Match Masters is a competitive match-3 game by Candivore. Instead of solving puzzles alone, you play live against another person: you both match coloured tiles on the same board style, and whoever scores more — helped by the booster you equipped — wins the round. Winning pays coins; losing costs you the booster you brought in.

The whole economy runs on coins and boosters. Boosters come in tiers — Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond and Legendary, plus rare Special Edition ones — and every booster you equip costs coins. A Diamond booster from the shop runs roughly 300–1,000 coins, which is why free coins matter so much: the better your booster, the better your odds, and the more coins you need to keep playing at that level. Stickers add a second layer — completing album pages pays coin bonuses and booster bundles, and teammates can trade duplicates. What is Coin Master?

Coin Master, by Moon Active, is a slot machine wrapped in a village-building game. You spin to earn coins, then spend those coins building and upgrading your village — each village has five items to build, and there are hundreds of villages to work through. Landing on attack lets you smash a building in another player's village for coins; landing on raid lets you dig up their stored coins. Shields absorb incoming attacks, and chests you buy with coins hold cards — completing card sets pays big rewards.

Pets add a wrinkle most new players miss: Foxy digs an extra spot during raids, Tiger adds coins to your attacks, and Rhino can block an incoming attack. Each pet only stays active for 4 hours after you feed it, so timing matters. The whole game runs on spins — every spin of the slot costs one, and your spin bar refills at 1 spin every 5 minutes up to a cap of 50. That cap is the reason free spin links matter so much: links stack on top of the cap, so a player who collects them can hold far more than 50 spins.

How free rewards work in Coin Master

1

Daily spin links

Moon Active posts free spin links on its official Facebook, Instagram, X and email channels almost every day — usually several per day, each worth 25–100 spins. Like Match Masters links, they expire in 24–72 hours and work once per account. A player who collects all of them can realistically pick up 50–300 bonus spins a day.

2

Hourly spin refill

Your spin bar refills on its own — 1 spin every 5 minutes, up to the 50-spin cap. The catch: it stops filling at the cap, so a full bar left overnight wastes spins. Spend down before you log off.

3

Friend gifts

You can send and receive spins with friends — a steady extra source on top of the links, as long as your friends actually play.

4

Events and village rewards

Rotating events like Viking Quest, Attack Madness and Raid Madness pay spins and coins for milestones, and completing a village pays spins plus a free chest. Saving a big pile of spins for an event — instead of burning them with nothing running — is the single best habit in the game.

On raw volume, Coin Master wins: more links per day, plus a refill that works while you sleep (until the cap). On reliability, Match Masters has an edge most players overlook — a skilled player manufactures their own coins by winning, while a Coin Master player who hits a dry spell on the slot has no way to force a payout. Links are the safety net in both games; skill is the engine in only one of them.

Skill vs luck

This is the real difference, and it decides which game you will actually enjoy six months from now. In Match Masters, a better player beats a worse player most of the time — tile strategy, booster choice and timing all matter, and the On Fire streak system rewards consistency. That is satisfying if you like competition, and frustrating if you just want to relax: every loss costs you the booster you equipped, so a bad session genuinely hurts.

Coin Master asks almost nothing of you. You spin, you watch, you collect. There is strategy in the margins — which pet to activate, when to spend spins, when to shield — but no spin is ever "played well". That makes it the better wind-down game, and the worse game if you want your decisions to matter. Neither is better in the abstract; they are opposite answers to the same question.

Progression: villages vs boosters

Coin Master progression is a straight line: earn coins, build the five items in your village, move to the next one, repeat across hundreds of villages. The number goes up, the villages get prettier, and the costs get bigger. It is satisfying in the same way filling a progress bar is satisfying — and it never really ends, because Moon Active keeps adding villages.

Match Masters progression is sideways as much as upwards. Your trophy count climbs when you win, but the real progression is your booster collection and your sticker albums — unlocking a Legendary booster or finishing an album page changes how you play, not just a number on a screen. The trade-off: progress can stall. A losing streak costs coins and boosters with nothing to show for it, while in Coin Master even a bad spin still pays some coins toward the next building.

🏆 Verdict: which is better for free players?

Pick Coin Master if you want the most free rewards for the least effort. More daily links, a spin bar that refills itself, and events that pay out for simply participating. You will never be blocked from playing — at worst you wait for the bar to refill.

Pick Match Masters if you want your skill to decide your income. A good player earns coins every match, doubles them on On Fire streaks, and turns tournament entries into profit. The ceiling is higher — but so is the floor: a bad day costs you boosters.

Our honest take: free players get the best deal from Coin Master's links and the most fun from Match Masters' matches. That is why so many players run both.

Can you play both?

Yes — and it is easier than it sounds, because the two games want different things from you. Coin Master takes a few minutes, several times a day: collect the links, spin the bar down, feed a pet, done. Match Masters takes focused sessions: a few matches when you have time to concentrate. The daily routines do not compete with each other, and the free-reward habits are identical in both — claim the official links every morning, because they expire in about 3 days and never come back.

One warning applies to both games equally: never pay for, download, or log into a "free coins" or "free spins" generator. Every single one is a scam — they harvest logins or install malware, and both Candivore and Moon Active will tell you the same. Real rewards only ever come from the game itself or its official social pages.

Match Masters vs Coin Master — FAQ

Which game gives more free rewards, Match Masters or Coin Master?

Coin Master posts more free links per day (several spin links, each worth 25–100 spins), while Match Masters typically posts a few gift links a day. But Match Masters rewards skill — a good player earns coins by winning — while Coin Master rewards are mostly luck from a slot machine. Volume favours Coin Master; control favours Match Masters.

Is Match Masters harder than Coin Master?

Yes, in the sense that winning depends on how you play. Match Masters is a head-to-head match-3 game against a real opponent, so skill, booster choice and timing decide matches. Coin Master is a slot machine — you spin and take what comes, so anyone can play it but nobody controls the outcome.

Do Coin Master free spin links expire?

Yes — most expire within 24 to 72 hours of being posted, the same as Match Masters gift links. Claim them the same day, on the phone where Coin Master is installed.

Can you play Match Masters and Coin Master at the same time?

Yes. They use different skills and different daily routines — Match Masters takes focused matches, Coin Master takes a few minutes of spinning and collecting. Many players keep both installed and collect free rewards from each every morning.

Are free coin and free spin generators real?

No. Every generator site for either game is a scam — they harvest logins or install malware. Real free rewards only come from the game itself or its official social pages.

Which game is better for free players?

If you want the most free stuff with the least effort, Coin Master — more daily links and a spin cap that refills on its own. If you want your skill to decide how much you earn, Match Masters — winning matches, On Fire streaks and tournaments pay real coins to good players.

Do Match Masters gift links work the same as Coin Master spin links?

Almost. Both are official links posted on the game's social pages, both expire in about 3 days, and both can be claimed once per account. The difference is the reward: Match Masters links give coins, boosters, stickers or perks; Coin Master links give spins (and sometimes coins).

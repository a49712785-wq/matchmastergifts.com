About Match Master Gifts

Match Master Gifts is an independent fan site with one job: publish verified daily gift links for the mobile game Match Masters — with honest timestamps, real check times, and automatic expiry. No dead links sitting in the "today" list. No fake "updated seconds ago" badges. No password harvesting, ever.

Why we exist

Gift-link pages are some of the most useful — and most abused — pages in mobile gaming. Too many of them republish links without checking, keep months of dead links live, or exist only to farm ad clicks. We built this site to be the page we'd want to bookmark ourselves: fast, honest, and gone-tomorrow accurate.

How we're run

A small team collects links from the game's official posts every morning, checks each one, and publishes them through an automated build. Our full process is documented on the How We Verify page — including the things we honestly can't verify.

Not affiliated

This is a fan project. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Candivore or Match Masters. All game names and trademarks belong to their owners.

How to redeem Match Masters gift links

Claiming a gift link takes about 30 seconds when everything works — and most "broken link" complaints are actually one of five fixable problems explained below.

The 30-second method

  1. Tap the gift link on the device where Match Masters is installed.
  2. The game opens by itself and a popup shows your reward.
  3. Tap Collect inside the game.
  4. Done — the reward is in your account instantly.

Fix every common error

1. Facebook error page opens instead of the game

Why: the link was opened on a desktop browser, or on a device where the game isn't installed / Facebook isn't connected to the game.

Fix: open the link on the same phone or tablet where Match Masters is installed. Then open the game → settings → make sure Facebook is connected. Tap the link again.

2. "Already claimed"

Why: each gift link works once per account. The game remembers your claim.

Fix: nothing is broken — just try a different link from today. Each one is a separate gift.

3. Link does nothing at all

Why: the link expired (older than ~3 days), or the game app is outdated and can't handle the redirect.

Fix: only use links from the last 3 days, and update Match Masters to the latest version from your app store.

4. "This link isn't available in your region"

Why: a small number of links are region-locked by the game maker.

Fix: you can't unlock these — but they're rare. The rest of today's links will still work for you.

5. Tapped Collect but no reward arrived

Why: usually a sync hiccup between the game and the server.

Fix: close the game completely and reopen it — rewards almost always appear after a restart. Check your booster inventory and coin balance before tapping the link again.

Pro routine (never miss a gift)

  • Claim new links within 24 hours of publishing — don't wait for day 3.
  • Use the same device every time so the game always opens correctly.
  • Keep the app updated; old versions break gift redirects first.
  • Bookmark our homepage — new verified links land there every morning.

Quick answers

Is it safe to tap these links?

Yes, when they come from official posts — like every link on our homepage. They open the game directly. Never enter your password on any "gift" site; real links never ask for it.

Can I claim the same link twice?

No — one claim per account per link. That's why we publish several fresh links every day instead of one.

Do links work on iPhone and Android?

Both, as long as the game is installed on the device you tap from.
